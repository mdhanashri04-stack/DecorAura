Our Heritage

Objects With A Story

Decor Aura was founded on a simple philosophy: home decor should exist at the intersection of sculpture, architectural function, and sensory warmth.

Artisanal Craftsmanship

Sculpted by Hand. Engineered for Precision.

Every object in our collection—from our iconic 3D-assembled Aurelia Lamp to monolithic Roman travertine coffee tables—is created in collaboration with master glassblowers and stone masons.

We reject mass-market plastic synthetic components in favor of solid brass, unglazed stoneware, mouth-blown opal glass, and sustainably harvested American walnut.
